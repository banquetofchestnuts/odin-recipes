Virgin Mojito

Description

Perfect balance of mint and lime with the option to make it a low sugar drink,
you'll be sipping this easy to make mocktail in less than 5 minutes!

Ingredients

  • Fresh Lime Juice
  • Honey Simple Syrup
  • Fresh Mint Leaves
  • Ice
  • Sparkling Water

Steps

  1. Place mint leaves and lime juice in a glass and muddle them together.
  2. Add the honey simple syrup.
  3. Add the sparkling water.
  4. Garnish with lime slices and serve.
